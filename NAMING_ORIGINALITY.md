# Nourally naming and originality screen

**Screening date:** August 4, 2026  
**Selected name:** Nourally  
**Pronunciation:** nur-AL-ee  
**Construction:** “nourish” + “ally”

## Recommendation

Nourally is the strongest screened name for this project. It expresses the product’s intended role—an approachable nutrition ally for student-athletes moving between school, training, and home—without describing a calorie counter or making a performance promise. The coined construction is more memorable and ownable than the former name, Fuel.

**Practical distinctiveness score: 9/10.** This is a product-naming screen, not a legal opinion or guarantee of trademark registrability.

## Evidence collected

### Indexed web and app-store screen

Exact-match searches for `“Nourally”` and `“Nourally” app`, plus exact searches limited to Apple’s App Store and Google Play, found no indexed nutrition, fitness, sports, wellness, or software product using the name. The few general-web matches were personal-name or archival/OCR references rather than relevant commercial brands.

This is materially stronger than Fuel, which is already used by multiple calorie and nutrition apps, including [Fuel: AI Nutrition](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/fuel-ai-nutrition/id6748624107) and [Fuel: Modern Calorie Tracking](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/fuel-modern-calorie-tracking/id1562577085).

## Why the name fits

- **Strategically relevant:** it conveys supportive nutrition guidance, not generic calorie counting.
- **Audience appropriate:** it sounds encouraging rather than clinical, punitive, or bodybuilding-focused.
- **Expandable:** it can cover food, hydration, scheduling, parent collaboration, grocery planning, and recovery.
- **Coined but understandable:** the two source ideas are recognizable while the combined word is unusual.
- **Searchable:** no relevant exact-match indexed commercial use was found in the screen.

## What “original” can and cannot mean

The evidence supports the narrower, defensible statement that **Nourally appears highly distinctive and no exact conflicting U.S. federal trademark, major app-store listing, relevant indexed commercial use, or registered `.com`, `.co`, or `.io` domain was found in the August 4, 2026 screen**.

No finite search can prove that a name is absolutely original worldwide. Unindexed businesses, state registrations, foreign marks, pending records not yet visible, common-law rights, and confusingly similar names may still exist. Before public launch or meaningful brand investment, commission a trademark attorney’s clearance search and file in the appropriate goods/services classes; secure the preferred domain and social handles promptly.
