## 3. Consistent, practical fueling recommendations

The timing engine already handles pre-activity, activity, recovery, travel, school access, and dietary/budget filters. Food → Meals already ranks candidates by pantry availability. Today still selects the first timing-filtered catalog idea before checking inventory for an existing plan.

- [x] Use one shared personalized meal-ranking path for Today and Food. Account for actual pantry quantities, food access, preferences, restrictions, and upcoming activity before choosing the primary suggestion. Clearly distinguish “available now” from “needs ingredients.” — Done (verified 2026.10.20): one path, `ideasFor()` → `rankIdeas()` in `src/domain/ranking.js` (pantry quantities, favorites, hidden ideas, food access, dislikes, diet, moment), used by Today (`timing.js`) and Ideas (`ideaMoments.js`); each idea shows what is at home and what is to buy. `tests/domain.test.js` P1-01 (two ranking tests); `tests/browser-journey.js` steps 6-7 (Today and Ideas pick the same first idea with the same availability).
- [x] Remove misleading default-food behavior, including automatically elevating “Banana + pretzels” solely because it is first in the catalog. Keep ordinary foods as valid options when they genuinely fit. When information or suitable food is missing, show a specific setup or shopping action instead of implying a fully personalized meal has been found. — Done (verified 2026.10.20): ranking puts ideas made from food at home first (with a bagel and jam at home, Bagel + jam ranks first; "Not a fan of bananas" removes Banana + pretzels). With nothing at home the first catalog idea still ties for first, but Today names every missing item ("Bananas to buy", "Pretzels to buy") with a Plan this button that adds them to groceries, and when no idea fits it gives the reason (`noIdeaReason`) and "Ideas for tomorrow" (`tests/browser-journey.js`, `tests/domain.test.js` P1-01). Checked in the running app and with `ideasFor()` directly.
- [x] Extend the existing hydration support into clear activity-timed guidance. Connect the relevant upcoming practice/game and supported duration/intensity context to preparation and hydration cues, not just an ounce log or generic reminder to drink. Keep logging optional and avoid independently prescribed fluid targets. — Done (verified 2026.10.20): hydration cues follow the next session in `src/domain/timing.js`: "Have a small, familiar snack now" and "Something small and easy, plus sips" before it, "Hydrate now; keep mid-session fuel familiar" during it (a 75-minute or high-intensity session gets the longer-session wording), "Sip water. Water logging is optional with no target or fraction (`WaterRow.jsx`, `tests/browser-units-week.js`).

## 5. Credible guidance and visible boundaries

An educational/non-medical disclaimer already exists on Profile, and meal guidance already warns that amounts are examples. Food database attribution is not the same as evidence supporting meal-timing or hydration rules.

- [x] Replace unsupported temporary recommendation assumptions with a documented, source-backed rule/content set. Associate meal-timing thresholds, recovery advice, hydration cues, and example portions with their supporting references. Do not treat USDA food composition data as endorsement of a fueling rule. — Done (verified 2026.10.20): `src/domain/rules.js` has one rule per Now card state (plus school window and game day), each with https sources and a checked date; thresholds the sources do not name (30/90/180 min, 75 min, 7:00 PM and others) are labelled "Nourally's own" (`tests/domain.test.js` P2-06 "every Now card state has a rule with https sources and a checked date").
- [x] Add accessible source and rationale details alongside recommendations, extending the existing “Why this action?” interaction. Clearly separate the reason this suggestion fits the athlete's schedule from the reference supporting the general guidance. — Done (verified 2026.10.20): Today › Why this? shows "Why it fits you" (the athlete's timing, access and food at home) apart from "What the tip is based on" (rule text, source links announced as opening in a new tab, "Source checked Oct 11, 2026") in every Now card state (`src/pages/Today/TodayPage.jsx`, `tests/browser-why.js`).
- [x] Surface the existing general-guidance boundary where advice is consumed, not only in Profile. Keep it concise and consistently state that Nourally supports fueling, timing, hydration, and planning—not medical treatment or independently prescribed calorie targets. Preserve the current non-prescriptive approach rather than adding a calorie-goal system. — Done (verified 2026.10.20): short boundary lines sit where advice is used (audit 7.5 tiers 2 and 3): Welcome "Nourally gives food ideas, not medical advice.", Ideas "Ideas are examples, not amounts you must eat.", Today › Water "Follow your coach's or doctor's plan if you have one.", and "Allergies: check every label." on every product view; the full text lives only in You › About Nourally's guidance.
